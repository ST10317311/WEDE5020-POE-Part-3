About Us

Who we are

The Drain Surgeon is a professional plumbing and drainage specialist dedicated to providing reliable solutions for homes and businesses across South Africa. With a focus on emergency plumbing and drain-related problems, our experienced teams are available around the clock to help customers get their plumbing systems back in working order.

From blocked drains and leaking pipes to geyset repairs and advanced drain inspections, we combine professional expertise with practical solutions to tackle plumbing problems efficiently.

Our Services

We provide a comprehensive range of plumbing and drainage services, including 24-hour emergency plumbing, blocked-drain solutions, geyser repairs and replacements, electronic leak detection, burst and leaking pipe repairs, CCTV camera drain inspections, high-pressure jetting, septic tanks and French drains.

Our aim is to provide customers with a convenient service that addresses the problem while helping identify its underlying cause.

Our Commitment

We believe that professional plumbing services should be reliable, responsive, and accessible when customers need them most. Our 24-hour emergency service is designed to provide assistance day or night, while our trained and security-cleared plumbers give customers confidence when inviting a professional into their homes or businesses.

At the Drain Surgeon, we are commited to delivering practical solutions, professional service, and a positive customer experience from the initial call through to the completion of the job.

Why Choose the Drain Surgeon?

  • 24-hour emergency plumbing services.
  • Experienced and security-cleared plumbers.
  • Professional drain and plumbing solutions.
  • Advanced diagnostic services such as electronic leak detection and CCTV drain inspections.
  • Services for both residential and commercial customers.
  • A 24-hour manned call centre for customer assistance.

Our Goal

Our goal is simple: to provide dependable plumbing and drainage solutions when South Africans need them the most.

Whether it is an unexpected blocked drain, a leaking pipe, a geyser problem, or a more complex drainage issue, The Drain Surgeon is there to help diagnose the problem and provide an effective solution.

"Any leak, burst pipe, or drain replacement, we are there for you." - The Drain Surgeon
